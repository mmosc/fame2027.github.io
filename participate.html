Rules & policies

Participation

  • Open to academic and industry researchers
  • Teams may consist of multiple members
  • One submission account per team

Model Constraints

  • A single model must be used for all task settings
  • Retraining per setting is not allowed

External Data

  • Use of external data must be clearly declared
  • Pretrained models are allowed unless otherwise stated

Ethical Use

  • Dataset is provided strictly for research purposes
  • Any misuse for surveillance or identity tracking is prohibited

Submission Rules

  • Limited number of submissions per day (15 submissions maximum)
  • Only the final submission will be used for ranking
  • Test labels are never released
